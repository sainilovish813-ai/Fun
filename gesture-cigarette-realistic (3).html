Gesture Cigarette FX — prototype (V-sign se cigarette, munh ke paas laa kar jalao)

loading model...
Do finger (V-sign / peace) dikhao → cigarette hath mein aa jayegi.
Pehli baar munh ke paas le jaane par vo nahi jalegi.
Munh se hatakar dobara paas le jao → thodi der baad jalegi 🔥
Jalne ke baad munh ke paas rakhne par smoke nahi aayegi — side mein karo aur hothon ka reaction do (jaise pi kar chhoda ho) → tabhi asli dhuaan (puff) nikalega 💨
